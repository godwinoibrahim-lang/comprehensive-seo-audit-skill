# Competitor Analysis Template

Systematic framework for analyzing SEO competitors and identifying opportunities.

## Phase 1: Identify Competitors

### Direct Competitors (Business)
Companies offering same services/products in your market:
- Same city/region
- Similar pricing
- Similar target audience

**How to find:**
- Google "[service] in [city]"
- Business associations/directories
- Client interviews ("Who else did you consider?")
- Social media/communities

### SEO Competitors (Search)
Sites ranking for your target keywords (may not be direct business competitors):
- National sites ranking locally
- Directories (Yelp, Thumbtack)
- Content aggregators
- Big brands with local presence

**How to find:**
- Google your target keywords, note top 10
- Ahrefs/Semrush Organic Competitors report
- Google Search Console — Performance tab → Queries → "Insights"
- "People also search for" suggestions

### Aspirational Competitors (Industry Leaders)
Top performers you want to emulate:
- National/international brands
- Highest authority sites in your niche
- Innovators in content/marketing

## Photography Industry Specific

### Local Competitors to Analyze

For wedding/portrait photographers:

1. **Ranking competitors**
   - "wedding photographer [city]"
   - "engagement photos [city]"
   - "portrait photographer near me"

2. **Direct business competitors**
   - Similar style/aesthetic
   - Similar pricing tier
   - Same venues/clients

3. **Industry sites (aspirational)**
   - Style Me Pretty
   - Junebug Weddings
   - Fearless Photographers

### What to Learn from Wedding Photography Competitors

| Aspect | What to Look For |
|--------|------------------|
| Portfolio Structure | Gallery organization, featured work |
| SEO Pages | Venue-specific pages, style pages |
| Blog Strategy | Real weddings, engagement sessions, vendor features |
| Link Building | Venue partnerships, vendor directories, publications |
| Social Integration | Instagram feeds, Pinterest strategy |
| Reviews/Trust | Testimonial placement, social proof |

### Competitive Advantages to Build

- **Hyper-local content** (neighborhood guides, venue-specific)
- **Niche specialization** (documentary, fine art, cultural)
- **Educational content** (planning guides, timeline advice)
- **Tools/resources** (budget calculators, vendor lists)
- **Featured publications** (real wedding submissions)
